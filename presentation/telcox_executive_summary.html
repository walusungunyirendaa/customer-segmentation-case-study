The problem

Broad campaigns are not paying off

TelcoX sends similar offers to almost everyone.

  • Average revenue per user is flat, and promotion spend is high.
  • Many customers stay on entry-level plans even though their usage suggests a bigger plan would fit.
  • Teams define a "high-value customer" in different ways.
The question: which customers are most ready to move to a higher plan, and what should each group be offered?
What we did

Grouped 15,000 customers by how they behave

Usage, spending, plan, and promotion history. No names or personal details.

1. Cleaned the data
  • 60 repeated records removed
  • 33 wrong plan records corrected
2. Grouped customers
  • K-means clustering on 19 behavior measures
  • Tested 2 to 10 groups and chose 5
3. Checked the result
  • At least 99.3% of customers kept their segment in 20 subsample tests
An honest note: customer behavior changes gradually, so the segments overlap. They are a practical way to divide customers, not natural separate groups.

Measuring success and next steps

Test it fairly before widening it

How we will measure
  • Upgrade rate and response rate
  • ARPU change
  • Cost per extra upgrade
  • Guardrails: opt-outs, complaints, disconnections
The fair test
  • Split eligible customers 50/50 at random
  • One half gets the segment offer, the other the usual offer
  • Three months, pooled across Segments 2 to 5 (5,786 customers)
Proposed targets
  • Upgrade rate at least 45% above control
  • Response rate at least 25% above control
  • Cost per upgrade at or below break-even
Next steps: 1. Confirm the targets. 2. Run the pilot, starting with Heavy Data Users. 3. Review at three months, and extend to six if the result is unclear. 4. Refresh the segments every quarter.
