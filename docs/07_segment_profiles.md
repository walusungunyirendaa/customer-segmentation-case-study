## 1. Purpose of This Document

This document describes the five customer segments found in the TelcoX customer data. It is written for marketing, sales, product, and customer care teams, so it uses plain language and avoids technical terms. It supports requirements FR9 and FR10 and user stories US5 and US6.

Each segment has its own page with a name, a short description, key facts, and a view of its upgrade potential. Ideas for offers, channels, and timing are in document 08, Marketing Recommendations.

**How the segments were made.** Customers were grouped by behavior only, meaning how they use TelcoX services and how they pay. Age, area, region, sign-up channel, and plan were not used to build the groups. They are used here to describe who is in each group.

**Numbering.** Segments are numbered from largest (Segment 1) to smallest (Segment 5).

## 4. Segment Pages

### Segment 1: Steady Postpaid Households

**34.2% of customers (5,137)**

**Summary.** The largest segment. These are postpaid customers who have been with TelcoX for about three and a half years, use moderate amounts of data and calls, and mostly stay within their plans.

| Key fact | This segment | All customers |
|---|---|---|
| Average monthly spend | 32.4 | 26.8 |
| Mobile data per month | 11.1 GB | 11.3 GB |
| Years as a customer | 3.5 | 2.9 |
| Has home broadband | 38.4% | 21.9% |
| On Plus or Premium plan | 50% | 25% |
| Customers over their data allowance | 9.8% | 28.0% |

**Who they are**
- Every customer in this segment is postpaid.
- They are spread across age groups much like all customers: 32% are aged 18 to 34 and 17% are 55 and over.
- 60% live in urban areas.
- Half are on a Plus or Premium plan, the highest share of any segment. Only 18% are on Basic.
- They mostly signed up in retail shops (42%).

**How they use TelcoX**
- Moderate data (median 8.6 GB) and about 275 call minutes a month.
- They spend the most of any segment, with Segment 3 close behind.
- They are the most likely to have home broadband. Those who do use about 121 GB a month on it.
- They have about 0.4 late payments in six months.

**Upgrade potential: rank 4 of 5 (score 19.3)**
- Most already have a plan that fits. 28% have outgrown their plan (over their data or call allowance and not on the top plan), and only 10% go over on data.
- 20.5% upgraded in the last 24 months, which is above average.
- They rarely accept promotions (9.2%).

**Points to keep in mind**
- This segment contains a smaller group of about 730 customers (14% of the segment) who roam abroad on three or more days in three months. They spend about 42.5 a month against 30.7 for the rest, and they use about 18 GB of data and 394 call minutes a month, against 10 GB and 255 minutes for the rest. A group like this formed its own segment in a six-segment test, but at 4.9% of customers it was just under the 5% minimum size rule.

### Segment 2: Light Prepaid Users

**27.0% of customers (4,051)**

**Summary.** Prepaid customers with light use and low spending. Most are on the Basic plan, and they are the least responsive to promotions and the least likely to have upgraded.

| Key fact | This segment | All customers |
|---|---|---|
| Average monthly spend | 19.9 | 26.8 |
| Mobile data per month | 4.6 GB | 11.3 GB |
| Top-ups per month | 1.8 | not applicable |
| Average value of one top-up | 13.1 | not applicable |
| Accepts promotions (average) | 4.9% | 12.5% |
| Upgraded in last 24 months | 9.4% | 17.6% |

**Who they are**
- Every customer is prepaid.
- 53% are on the Basic plan and 31% on Standard.
- 31% are aged 18 to 34 and 17% are 55 and over.
- 52% live in urban areas.
- They mostly signed up in retail shops (41%) or through resellers (26%).

**How they use TelcoX**
- Low data (median 3.0 GB) and about 160 call minutes a month.
- About two top-ups a month, and each top-up is larger than in Segment 3.
- Few bundles (0.7 in three months).
- The typical customer uses about a third of their data allowance.

**Upgrade potential: rank 5 of 5 (score 4.2)**
- 34% have outgrown their plan (over their data or call allowance and not on the top plan), mostly on call minutes. Only 11% go over on data.
- Past upgrades and response to promotions are both the lowest of any segment.

**Points to keep in mind**
- Age is not stated for 18% of this segment, so the age shares above are lower than the true shares.

### Segment 3: Deal-Seeking Prepaid Users

**18.3% of customers (2,749)**

**Summary.** Almost entirely prepaid, with frequent small top-ups and many bundle purchases. They respond to promotions more than any other segment, and they are the newest customers.

| Key fact | This segment | All customers |
|---|---|---|
| Top-ups per month | 4.7 | not applicable |
| Bundles bought in 3 months | 5.0 | 2.0 |
| Accepts promotions (average) | 27.4% | 12.5% |
| Years as a customer | 1.8 | 2.9 |
| Aged 18 to 34 | 50% | 37% |
| Customers over their data allowance | 42.5% | 28.0% |

**Who they are**
- 99.7% are prepaid.
- Half are aged 18 to 34, and only 5% are 55 and over.
- 58% live in urban areas.
- They sign up through a mix of channels: the app (29%), resellers (28%), and self-service (22%). Retail shops are used least (21%).
- Plans are spread out: 37% Basic, 38% Standard, and 25% Plus or Premium.

**How they use TelcoX**
- Many small top-ups, about 8.9 each.
- They send the most texts of any segment (137 a month) and buy the most bundles.
- Data use is average to high (12.3 GB, median 8.8 GB), and they went over their data limit in 1.2 of the last 3 months on average.
- Despite being prepaid, they spend almost as much as Segment 1 (31.9). The money comes in through many small top-ups and bundles.

**Upgrade potential: rank 3 of 5 (score 54.6)**
- 51% have outgrown their plan (over their data or call allowance and not on the top plan).
- 19.7% upgraded in the last 24 months.
- Their willingness to respond to offers is the strongest of any segment.

**Points to keep in mind**
- Age is not stated for 18% of this segment, so the youth share is probably higher than shown.
- They are the newest customers, so they have the shortest history with TelcoX.

### Segment 4: Heavy Data Users

**12.3% of customers (1,848)**

**Summary.** The heaviest data users by a wide margin. Nearly all of them use more data than their plan allows, almost every month, and nearly all are on a Basic or Standard plan.

| Key fact | This segment | All customers |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile data per month | 28.8 GB | 11.3 GB |
| Customers over their data allowance | 98.6% | 28.0% |
| On Basic or Standard plan | 97% | 73% |
| Average monthly spend | 25.0 | 26.8 |
| Upgraded in last 24 months | 28.4% | 17.6% |
| Aged 18 to 34 | 60% | 37% |

**Who they are**
- A mix of account types: 60% postpaid and 40% prepaid.
- The youngest segment: 60% are aged 18 to 34 and only 6% are 55 and over.
- The most urban segment (72%).
- The most likely to have signed up through the app (42%).
- 65% are on Basic, 31% on Standard, and almost none on Plus or Premium.

**How they use TelcoX**
- About 29 GB of data a month, which is more than two and a half times the average customer. The typical customer uses over three times their data allowance.
- They went over their data limit in nearly every month (2.9 of the last 3).
- Half of their usage happens at off-peak times, the highest share of any segment.
- Call use is below average (186 minutes) and text use is above average (112).

**Upgrade potential: rank 1 of 5 (score 91.3)**
- 98.6% use more than their plan allows and are not on the top plan.
- They have the highest rate of past upgrades (28.4%) and respond to promotions at a good level (20.0%).
- The top rank holds under equal weights. Under a ranking on need alone, they come second by a small margin.

**Points to keep in mind**
- Their spending (25.0) is below the all-customer average even though they use far more data. This suggests they sit on plans that are cheap for how much they use.

### Segment 5: Long-Term Voice Users

**8.1% of customers (1,215)**

**Summary.** The smallest segment. These customers make about three times as many calls as the average customer, use little data, have been with TelcoX the longest, and skew older.

| Key fact | This segment | All customers |
|---|---|---|
| Call minutes per month | 757 | 252 |
| Customers over their call allowance | 100% | 35.8% |
| Years as a customer | 4.7 | 2.9 |
| Aged 55 and over | 33% | 15% |
| Average monthly spend | 17.7 | 26.8 |
| Accepts promotions (average) | 7.1% | 12.5% |

**Who they are**
- Mostly postpaid (73%).
- The oldest segment: 33% are aged 55 and over, and only 16% are aged 18 to 34.
- The least urban segment (50%).
- More than half signed up in retail shops (51%), the highest share of any segment.
- 59% are on Basic and 34% on Standard.

**How they use TelcoX**
- About 757 call minutes a month. The typical customer uses more than four times their call allowance.
- Low data use (median 3.7 GB), and the lowest share of off-peak use (26%).
- The lowest average spend of any segment (17.7).
- The fewest late payments (about 0.3 in six months).

**Upgrade potential: rank 2 of 5 (score 56.1)**
- They have the strongest need of any segment (99.3%), because nearly all exceed their call allowance.
- Past upgrades (12.2%) and response to promotions (7.1%) are low.
- Their rank depends on the weights. They come first on need alone, and third under equal weights.

**Points to keep in mind**
- A strong need combined with weak response suggests this group may be harder to reach through promotions than through other routes.

## 5. Using These Profiles With Care

- **The segments overlap.** Customer behavior changes gradually, so there are no clear gaps between groups. A customer near the edge of one segment could fit another. The silhouette score for five segments was 0.157, which is low. Use the segments as a practical way to divide customers, not as exact labels.
- **The segments are stable.** In 20 tests on random subsamples, at least 99.3% of customers stayed in the same segment.
- **Age is incomplete.** Age is not stated for 11% of all customers and for 18% of Segments 2 and 3, so age shares are understated there.
- **Region does not separate the segments.** Each region makes up almost the same share of every segment, so region is not used in the profiles.
- **Upgrade scores depend on the weights.** The weights are a judgment call. Segment 4 ranks first in two of the three weightings tested, and Segments 1 and 2 are always in the bottom two.
- **Names and numbers match the seed 42 data.** If the data is regenerated with a different seed, the numbering and numbers can change.
- **The data is synthetic.** These profiles show how the method works. They do not describe real TelcoX customers.
